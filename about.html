Our Story

Who We Are

A Legacy Carved in Stone

Founded in 1999, Gwalior Stone Crafts began as a modest quarry operation in Rajasthan. Today, with an annual turnover of $45 million USD and over 6,000 containers exported per year, we are one of India's most celebrated natural stone companies.

Our passion for stonework runs deep — from the ancient quarries of Rajasthan to luxury interiors in Europe, the Middle East, Australia and beyond. Every slab, tile and crafted piece bears our commitment to quality, sustainability and artisanship.

1999

The Beginning

Gwalior Stone Crafts was founded in Jaipur, Rajasthan with a vision to bring India's finest stones to the global market. Our first export consignment shipped to the United Kingdom.

2005

Global Expansion

We expanded into 20+ countries, established dedicated sales offices in Europe and the Middle East, and launched our luxury marble collection for high-end residential projects.

2012

ISO Certification & Green Energy

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certifications. Installed wind turbines at our processing plant — a first for the Indian stone industry.

2024

$45M Milestone

Reached $45 million annual turnover, serving 50+ countries with 6,000+ containers. Launched the Pietra Dura luxury inlay collection, celebrating India's decorative stone heritage.
